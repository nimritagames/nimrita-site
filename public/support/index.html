Reporting a bug

Three things make a bug fixable on the first try:

  • Which game, and the version number from the Play Store listing
  • Your phone model and Android version
  • What you did just before it went wrong

A screenshot or screen recording helps more than any description.

Lost progress

Game progress is saved on your device, not on a server, so uninstalling a game or switching phones will not carry it across. Unfortunately that also means lost progress cannot be restored from my side — there is no copy of it anywhere but your device.

Refunds

Purchases and refunds are handled entirely by Google Play, not by me. Use Google's refund process, which is faster than going through me.
