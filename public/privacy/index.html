Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026. This policy covers every Nimrita Games app published on Google Play.

The short version

Nimrita Games is a one-person studio. The games do not ask you to create an account, and I have no interest in collecting your personal information.

Information the games collect

The games do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Game progress — levels completed, settings, high scores — is stored only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Information collected by Google

The games are distributed through Google Play. Google collects some information independently of me, such as crash reports and basic, aggregated statistics about installs and device types. I can see these only as anonymous totals in the Play Console; they do not identify you.

Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and analytics

The games currently contain no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics SDKs. If that changes in a future release, this policy will be updated before that release goes live, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety section on Google Play.

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them. Since no personal information is collected from anyone, there is nothing held about a child to request deletion of.

Your data, and removing it

Because game data never leaves your device, I hold nothing to export or delete. To remove everything a game has stored, uninstall it. To ask about data Google holds, use Google's own privacy controls.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version appears on this page with a new date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the relevant app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about any of the games: kamshu@nimritagames.com.
